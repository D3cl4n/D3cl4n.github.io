About

Hello, my name is Declan Murphy! I have my BS/MS from Worcester Polytechnic Institute and I currently work as a computer scientist. Since graduation I have maintained my interest in applied cryptography and want to continue my research in zero-knowledge proofs, privacy-preserving systems, and media integrity/authenticity. I am motivated by open problems such as verifiable chains of trust regarding media and the ability of zk-SNARKs to meet these needs. I try and start from the problem and then work back to the engineering solution, creating natural motivation.

  • Your current position: Computer Scientist/Engineer
  • Research interests: zk-SNARKs for media integrity/authenticity
  • Long-term goals: scaling current research to large images and videos
  • Current collaborations: engaged with ZKHACK and Cryptohack communities onine
