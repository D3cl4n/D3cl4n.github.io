Notes on Binary Tower Fields and STARKs (1)

STARKs formed over towers of binary fields have been proven to be incredibly efficient for producing proofs for traditional hash functions such as SHA-256 and BLAKE3. I am motivated to learn how these STARKs work so that I can apply this efficiency to any computation trace that can be arithmetized into bitwise operations (and other arithmetic operations) over towers of binary fields.

1. The Base Field \( \mathbb{F}_{2} \)

The base field \( \mathbb{F}_{2} \) has two elements \( \{0, 1\} \), and is equipped with two binary operations \( (\oplus, \cdot) \) which are functionally equivalent to addition and multiplication.

The Pythagorean theorem is \( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \).

2. Display Mathematics

The integral formula:

$$ \int_a^b f(x)\,dx = F(b) - F(a) $$

The quadratic formula:

$$ x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} $$
