1. Inline Math Example

You can write math right inside your sentences. For example, the famous equation \( E = mc^2 \) shows the relationship between mass and energy. Another example is finding the hypotenuse of a triangle using \( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \).

2. Block / Display Math Example

For larger, more complex formulas, use the block syntax to place them on their own centered line:

$$ \int_{a}^{b} f(x) \,dx = F(b) - F(a) $$

Here is another block equation showing the quadratic formula:

$$ x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} $$
